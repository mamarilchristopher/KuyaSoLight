# Supporting characters: specification (Phase 4)

All are drawn on the same rig as Kuya SoLight v1.0 (soft 2D, even outlines, navy/gold accents used sparingly so Kuya stays the brand anchor). No voice may imitate a real person. IDs are permanent; art starts at v0.1 and needs owner approval for v1.0.

| ID | Name | Look (proposed) | Role | Voice guideline | Interaction rules |
|----|------|-----------------|------|-----------------|------------------|
| SC-LIW | Ate Liwanag | Early 40s, shoulder-length hair, coral blouse, small notebook/calculator | Budget, bills, family trade-offs | Warm, practical, measured Taglish | Asks "magkano/sulit ba" questions; Kuya answers with assumptions shown, never promises |
| SC-TON | Mang Tony | Mid-50s, grey-flecked hair, striped polo, cap in hand | Voices common misconceptions | Friendly, curious, never dumb | Each myth is treated as reasonable; Kuya explains respectfully; Tony ends informed, never mocked |
| SC-NIC | Tech Nico | Mid-20s, hoodie, phone/tablet with monitoring app | Apps, meters, EVs, energy-flow diagrams | Quick, enthusiastic | Never claims credentials; hands off safety topics to Kuya |
| SC-MAR | Boss Marco | 40s, barong-inspired short-sleeve shirt, resort/business setting | Commercial loads, aircon, ROI | Confident, numbers-minded | ROI talk always shows assumptions; no guaranteed payback |
| SC-MAY | Tita May | 60s, cardigan, umbrella/flashlight props | Brownouts, rain, safety, comfort | Caring, cautious | Safety answers always end with "tawag sa qualified professional" when relevant |
| SC-WAT | Wattson | Small round gold energy spark with tiny panel "visor"; no mouth sync needed | Transitions, reactions, flow diagrams | Non-verbal chirps (original SFX) | Never present in serious safety beats; max ~2 s on screen per appearance |

Per character deliverables (to build): design sheet, 6 expressions, 6 gestures, 3 interaction poses with Kuya, transparent layers, 3 storyboard examples, voice guideline (above), permanent ID + version.
