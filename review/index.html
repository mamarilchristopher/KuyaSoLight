The four original directions

A · Friendly Solar ExpertB · Modern 3D PresenterC · Illustrated Tech EducatorD · Casual Filipino Kuya
KSL-A

A · Friendly Solar Expert

Premium soft-vector 2D presenter. Rounded shapes, expressive eyes, navy polo with gold collar and sleeve trims. Clean, even line weight.

Strengths

  • Easiest to animate consistently (flat layers, few gradients)
  • Reads clearly at phone size
  • Warm and approachable without looking childish

Risks

  • Can look generic if expressions are under-used
  • Needs a disciplined colour/line spec to stay premium

Production

Setup
3–5 days to lock full bundle
Per episode
Low (code-driven poses)
Reuse
Very high
Consistency
Very high (same vector parts every frame)

Rig: Layered SVG cut-out rig (this repo): bone angles for arms/legs, 6-viseme mouth set, blink and brow controls. Portable to Rive or Moho later.

KSL-B

B · Modern 3D Presenter

Stylised "3D-look" presenter: large head, volumetric gradients, specular eye highlights, thin navy glasses, zip jacket over a cream tee. This is a 2.5D vector mock of the direction; a true 3D version needs a modelled and rigged asset.

Strengths

  • Feels premium and modern
  • Lighting gives depth that stands out in feed
  • Glasses read as "smart" without credentials

Risks

  • True 3D needs a Blender/Unreal model + rig (extra cost and time)
  • Highest risk of inconsistency if generated by AI image/video tools
  • Heavier renders

Production

Setup
2–4 weeks (modelling, texturing, rig, lookdev)
Per episode
Medium–High
Reuse
High once built
Consistency
High only with a real 3D model; Low with generative tools

Rig: Production: Blender armature + shape keys for visemes/expressions, rendered to image sequences; or Ready-made stylised rig. Prototype here: 2.5D gradient SVG on the shared rig.

KSL-C

C · Illustrated Tech Educator

Flat editorial illustration: no outlines, small head on a tall frame, big expressive hands, minimal dot-eye face. Built to sit inside dense diagrams.

Strengths

  • Best for information-dense reels (character never fights the diagram)
  • Fast to pose; strong silhouette at tiny sizes
  • Very cheap to render

Risks

  • Less facial warmth; relies on gesture and voice
  • Can feel corporate if overused

Production

Rig: Flat SVG/Lottie-style bone rig with large hand library (12 gesture swaps). Easy export to Lottie for web.

KSL-D

D · Casual Filipino Kuya

Grounded homeowner-turned-solar-enthusiast. Near-human proportions, light stubble, plain navy cap (no logo), open navy overshirt with gold piping over a cream tee, jeans and white sneakers.

Strengths

  • Most relatable and "real kuya" in feel
  • Credible next to real project footage (Template 06)
  • Less mascot-like

Risks

  • More detail = more animation cost per expression
  • Must avoid resembling any real person
  • Uncanny if over-detailed

After you pick, the chosen direction gets the full locked bundle: side profile, 16 expressions, 12 gestures, 8 body poses, 12 mouth shapes, walk/sit/present loops and a 10-frame consistency test, versioned and owner-approved.
